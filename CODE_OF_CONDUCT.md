# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a small project about Arabic-language tooling,
and it should be a place where Arabic speakers, learners, and people who have
never worked with RTL text all feel able to ask questions without being made to
feel stupid for asking.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are technical and resolvable with an
  example, a test, or a clip that reproduces the problem.
- Critique the code, not the person. "This breaks shaping" is useful; "this is
  wrong" is not.
- Be patient with questions about the language itself. Not everyone working on
  this speaks Arabic, and not every Arabic speaker has worked with bidi text —
  both are fine.
- Respect that contributors are doing this in their own time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commit
  messages, or anywhere else the project lives.
- Discrimination on the basis of nationality, ethnicity, religion, language,
  dialect, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or
  appearance.
- Dismissing someone's dialect, spelling, or variety of Arabic as "not proper" —
  the project exists because Arabic is spoken in many forms.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer,
[@abdeljalilELmajjodi](https://github.com/abdeljalilELmajjodi), rather than in a
public thread. Reports will be read and acted on, and the reporter's privacy
will be respected.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts for behaviour
that violates the above, and will explain the reason where it is useful to do so.
